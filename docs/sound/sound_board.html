Breakfast Run Sound Board

The sounds of one race on the group C tape, recorded from the emulator as the Spectrum’s beeper plays them. The strip under each shows how busy the sound is: one bar a fiftieth of a second, taller for more speaker clicks.

To make the sounds count for more

  1. Skid screech. There is none on this tape: the screech belongs to the old handling model. A screech while a car slides, harsher the further over its grip.
  2. Heavier crash. Twice as long, denser noise, and longer still for a hard hit than a rub.
  3. Engine with more body. More clicks a second, rising in pitch with speed, so you hear yourself accelerate.
  4. Oil spin sound. Its own sound: a whooshing fall in pitch through the spin, not the crash.
  5. NOS roar. A rough roar for the whole burn, not only the moment it fires.
  6. Overtaking. A short rising blip when you pass a car and a falling one when you are passed, with the faces.
  7. Final lap. A bigger fanfare as you start the last lap.

Every sound shares the processor with the picture, so each one is tried on the tape and measured for smoothness before it stays.
