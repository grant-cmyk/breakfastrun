Breakfast Run HUD layouts

Four places the race information could go, each drawn as a real ZX Spectrum screen: the real race frame from the game, the ROM's own letters, the real car pictures, the Spectrum's 15 colours, one ink and one paper per 8x8 cell. Colour is free outside the scrolling window, because those cells never move.

24 x 16 cellsToday: 192 x 128 px. 75% of the width, 67% of the height, 50.0% of the screen.
23 x 16 cellsOne character narrower: 184 x 128 px, 47.9% of the screen. Cannot be centred (4 cells one side, 5 the other), so it suits a side panel.
22 x 16 cellsOne character off each side: 176 x 128 px, 45.8% of the screen. Centred.

A. Bottom strip

Window 22 x 16, centred. HUD: one line under the window.

  • Closest to what the game has now; the least work.
  • Shows lap, place and time, but not who is ahead of you.

B. Position tower

Recommended

Window 23 x 16 at the left. HUD: a 6-cell panel on the right.

  • Uses the one-character-narrower window as asked, and the space it frees.
  • The race order with every car's own picture, the way Micro Machines shows it. Each car gets a colour, so you can tell them apart even though they are black on the track.
  • Your row is red and moves as you pass or get passed.

C. Top banner

Window 22 x 16, moved down 2 rows. HUD: a banner above it.

  • A big, double-size place you can read at a glance.
  • Room for a best-lap time.
  • No race order.

D. Wings

Window 22 x 16, centred. HUD: lap and time on the left, the race order on the right.

  • Symmetrical, and keeps the race order.
  • Both wings are narrow (4-5 cells), so the text is tight.

Pick: B, the position tower

It is the layout that makes the narrower window pay: the freed column and the panel beside it show the whole field in race order, with each car's own picture. It is the one that feels like Micro Machines.

Speed: a narrower window also means less to draw each picture. I estimate about 5k T-states saved at 23 cells and about 11k at 22 (scaled from the measured tile, copy and clear costs; not yet measured). Either is enough to remove most of the slowdown off the start with five cars. The HUD itself costs almost nothing: it is redrawn only when a number changes. The time and best-lap figures are proposals; the game has no timer yet.
