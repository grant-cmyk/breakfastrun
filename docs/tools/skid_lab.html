When a slide feels right, copy the settings and paste them into the chat. Claude builds that model into the game.

How the grip model works

  1. Each frame the car's speed is split two ways: forwards (where it points) and sideways.
  2. The engine and drag only change the forwards part: top speed is the same as the game's, 1.75 px a frame.
  3. The tyres cancel sideways speed, but only up to their grip each frame. Under that, all of it goes and the car follows its nose.
  4. Steer faster than the grip can follow and the car slides. Sliding tyres hold less than gripping ones, so the slide goes on and fades out gradually.
  5. The more you steer, the more it slides; ease off, or steer the other way, and it gathers itself up. Holding Q uses up part of the grip as well, so power in a corner swings the back out.
