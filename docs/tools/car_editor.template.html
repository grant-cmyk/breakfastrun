A face is one colour on the driver's band in the race order, exactly as here: Ink draws, Clear (or right-click) shows the band. Mirror paints both halves at once. Faces show beside the cars before the race starts.

Ink is black. Paper is the car's white body: solid, so the road does not show through it. Clear leaves the road showing. Right-click also clears. Mirror paints both sides of the car at once, on the yellow line (straight up and 45° only). At straight up it can flip the checker on a tyre, because the checker does not mirror.
