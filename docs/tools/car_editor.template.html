A face is one colour on the driver's band in the race order, exactly as here: left-click draws Ink; right-click or double-click shows the band. Mirror paints both halves at once. Faces show beside the cars before the race starts.

The big cars are in the game. 20 × 20: straight up is your drawing; 22½° and 45° are Claude's drafts from it, to touch up - the game makes the other 13 headings from these three (shown on the right). The 16 × 16 car at the same heading is shown beside it. Left-click draws Ink, right-click Paper, double-click clears. Mirror paints both sides on the yellow line - the bodies are an odd width (centre column 10), so the number sits dead centre; column 20 is left empty.

Ink is black. Paper is the car's white body: solid, so the road does not show through it. Clear leaves the road showing. Left-click draws Ink, right-click Paper, double-click clears (drag to paint a run). Mirror paints both sides of the car at once, on the yellow line (straight up and 45° only). At straight up it can flip the checker on a tyre, because the checker does not mirror.
