BREAKFAST RUN CAR ART v1
X = ink (black)   o = paper (white body)   . = empty (road shows)
Hand-drawn cars. make_data.py uses these in place of its own drawing for any
car named here. Heading 0 of each: drawn by Grant, 9 Nov 2026 (YOU his second
car, CYAN his first (redrawn in the editor), PINK, WHITE and GREEN drawn in the car
editor, 10 Nov 2026).
22.5 and 45: Claude, rebuilt from his shapes at the angle (one-bit-art skill,
rule T1; PINK and WHITE sampled 16 times a pixel, 45 made symmetric), drafts
for him to check.
