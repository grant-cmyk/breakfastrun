BREAKFAST RUN CAR ART v1
X = ink (black)   o = paper (white body)   . = empty (road shows)
Hand-drawn cars. GREEN is the clown car (Grant's heading 0 from an editor screenshot,
10 Nov 2026; 22.5 and 45 turned about the car's own centre). The 6-wheeled truck it
replaced is TRUCK in tools/pool_art.txt.
 WHITE is Slim Jane (Grant's heading 0, 10 Nov 2026; her 22.5 and 45
were placed by hand from it). The driver she replaced is kept in tools/pool_art.txt.
 make_data.py uses these in place of its own drawing for any
car named here. Heading 0 of each: drawn by Grant, 9 Nov 2026 (YOU his second
car, CYAN his first (redrawn in the editor), PINK, WHITE and GREEN drawn in the car
editor, 10 Nov 2026).
22.5 and 45: Claude, rebuilt from his shapes at the angle (one-bit-art skill,
rule T1; sampled 16 times a pixel, 45 made symmetric; PINK's tracked car
rebuilt from its key shapes - tracks, body, cockpit ring, barrel - as its tread
pattern does not survive turning), drafts for him to check.
