== MAX / RACER ==
acceleration 5, speed 5, grip 4, armour 5
behaviour: The all-rounder. Nothing stands out and nothing lets it down. Slides in a way you can read and catch.
note: Our racer - an all rounder, although grip is probably its weakenss

== FRANK / COUPE ==
acceleration 5, speed 5, grip 4, armour 5
behaviour: The racer. Keeps a tidy line and brakes late. A little more top speed than Max, and the best of the low-grip bunch through a bend.
note: very similar to Max

== JANE / DRAGSTER ==
acceleration 7, speed 8, grip 1, armour 2
behaviour: Fastest in a straight line by a distance. Brakes early because she has to: the long car hates a tight corner and skids wide if pushed.
note: A dragster but terrible on turns, should shoot ahead of Max on the straights but really battle on the corners

== BRUNO / TANK ==
acceleration 4, speed 2, grip 8, armour 9
behaviour: Heavy and slow, likes to get in the way. Cuts corners so minimise track time to save
note: A tank. Really slow but excellent grip

== SPIKE / CLOWN CAR ==
acceleration 6, speed 2, grip 3, armour 4
behaviour: Erratic. A real clown. unpredictable. Almost seems to want to hit other cars
note: Great acceleration but not much speed - reasonable grip

== SPECS / SALOON ==
acceleration 4, speed 5, grip 3, armour 5
behaviour: A decently fast car - good acceleration, good speed  and reasonable grip - a real challenger
note: -
