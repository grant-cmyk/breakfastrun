BREAKFAST RUN - THE CARS YOU CAN CHOOSE (the CHOOSE YOUR CAR popup: PICK=1, src/pick.asm)
Grant, 23 Nov 2026: "when you click 1, a popup appears asking you to choose your car. You press left and right to select
cars"; "Everything can be adjusted but the armour for a car is fixed ... when you choose a car at the beginning there is no
option to adjust anything. The car has default settings."; "I made those changes in each car so that only my car gets
those changes" (his versions: tools/car_art_studio.txt).

One block a car, in the order the popup shows them (the first is the one you start with). To change a word, change it here
and build again (bash tools/build_race.sh).
  art:    the drawing's name (tools/car_art.txt / pool_art.txt; YOUR version from tools/car_art_studio.txt if it is there)
  card:   the driver card its ratings come from (tools/driver_cards.txt): acceleration, speed, grip, armour
  colour: the band behind it in the popup - red, cyan, green, magenta, yellow, white
  weight: its weight class in a bump (0 light ... 2 heavy; src/weight.asm)
  line, good, poor: the popup's words - up to 22 letters for the line, 20 for good and poor
