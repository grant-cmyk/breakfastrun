Breakfast Run: three new cars

Three empty slots for cars to choose from. Draw each car facing up, 16 pixels square, the same size as the cars in the race. Ink is the dark line, Body is the white inside, Rub out lets the road show. "Start from the red car" drops your own car in as a size guide. When you are done, copy the text and paste it back to Claude; Claude turns each car to its other headings.
