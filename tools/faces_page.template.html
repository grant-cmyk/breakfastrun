Breakfast Run: the drivers' faces

Each driver six ways: as in the game now; with the eyes moved; happy; sad; and two new ones, idea 1 and idea 2, for trying other expressions (they start as copies of now). Under each name the podium animation plays. Click a square to swap it: paper becomes ink, ink becomes paper; drag to paint the same way. The arrows under each face shift the whole picture one pixel (what falls off one edge comes back on the other); Mirror flips it left to right; Copy takes a face and Paste puts it over another (any driver, any look). Undo takes back the last change. When you are happy, copy the text and paste it back to Claude.
