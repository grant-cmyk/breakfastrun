Breakfast Run: the mug's steam

The steaming mug has two pictures, and the game swaps between them. Only the top three rows (the steam) differ: paint them in each frame and watch the animation. Painting the mug itself, below the steam, changes both frames. When you are happy, copy the text and paste it back to Claude.

The animation

In the game the swap comes about twice a second (the middle of the slider). I have not timed it on a real machine.
